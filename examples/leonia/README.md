# Two stories from the Leonia runs

Before the atelier lived in a real cluster, it lived through synthetic years of
Leonia, a simulated cluster with a real load history. These two stories come
from those runs. They are kept here as written; nothing was edited.

- `raspustit.ru.txt` — «Распустить», by Тихон Молчанов, written in Russian.
  It is the first work in «Поэтика непорученного», a school Molchanov founded
  himself on the seventh night; its manifesto is in
  `poetika-neporuchennogo.ru.md`. The variants after the last `***` are the
  author's own alternative passages, kept with the text.
- `der-lockere-knopf.de.txt` — «Der lockere Knopf», by Eberhard Falkenstein,
  written in German in the school of minimalism, and
  `der-lockere-knopf.ru.txt`, its Russian translation by the atelier's
  Translator.
